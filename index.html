What this is

Builders Breakdown is a show about the people who build things, not just the things they build. Every episode is a conversation in the shop, at the track, or under the truck — the fabrication choices, the failures, and the reason someone spent eighteen months on a project that only runs for four hours a year.

Got a build worth featuring, or a story that fits? Send it over — see the contact section below.
